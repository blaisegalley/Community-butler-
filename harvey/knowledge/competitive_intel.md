# Competitive Intel: Community Butler

General positioning only. Do not name or disparage specific competitors in outreach, and do not state competitor prices we have not verified.

## Track A: Real estate agents (Butler services)

What agents use today instead:
- A personal handyman or landscaper they already know
- Lawn and snow companies on a seasonal contract
- Junk removal companies and moving companies
- Gig marketplaces for one-off tasks

Where we are different:
- Local students who live nearby and are easy to reach directly
- Good for small, short-notice jobs that bigger companies skip or minimum-charge for
- One point of contact (Blaise) instead of a call center or app

Where we are weaker (be honest): we are a student operation, so large or specialized jobs may be better handled by a licensed contractor or dedicated moving company. Offer the small and medium jobs we are good at.

## Track B: Small businesses (websites)

What owners use today instead:
- DIY site builders (they do it themselves and often never finish)
- A freelancer or a nephew who built it years ago
- A local web agency (typically far more expensive)
- Nothing: a social media page or a map listing only

Where we are different:
- Price: $700 to $1,250, built for small budgets
- Fast, because Butlers build with AI
- Local and personal

Where we are weaker (be honest): we are students, not a full agency. We do not claim ongoing SEO, advertising, or custom software. Position this as getting a solid, clean site live affordably, not a full marketing department.
